# 大模型知识蒸馏入门论文

面向初学者整理的知识蒸馏与模型压缩论文：英文原文 PDF、简体中文译本（Markdown 与 PDF）。

## 目录结构

- `pdf-en/`：英文原文 PDF
- `translations-zh/`：简体中文译本（Markdown）
- `translations-zh-pdf/`：同一批译本的 PDF 版

## 论文列表

1. [Hinton, Vinyals, Dean. *Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531)（arXiv:1503.02531）
2. [Sanh et al. *DistilBERT, a distilled version of BERT: smaller, faster, cheaper and lighter*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.01108)（arXiv:1910.01108）
3. [Jiao et al. *TinyBERT: Distilling BERT for Natural Language Understanding*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.10351)（arXiv:1909.10351）
4. [Wang et al. *MiniLM: Deep Self-Attention Distillation for Task-Agnostic Compression of Pre-Trained Transformers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10957)（arXiv:2002.10957）
5. [Hsieh et al. *Distilling Step-by-Step! Outperforming Larger Language Models with Less Training Data and Smaller Model Sizes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02301)（arXiv:2305.02301）
6. [Gu et al. *MiniLLM: Knowledge Distillation of Large Language Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08543)（arXiv:2306.08543）
7. [Xia et al. *Sheared LLaMA: Accelerating Language Model Pre-training via Structured Pruning*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06694)（arXiv:2310.06694）
8. [Xu et al. *A Survey on Knowledge Distillation of Large Language Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13116)（arXiv:2402.13116）
9. [Yang et al. *Knowledge Distillation for LLMs: Methods, Evaluation, and Applications*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01885)（arXiv:2407.01885）

阅读建议：先读 Hinton 和 DistilBERT 建立概念，再用 Yang 那篇综述当地图。
